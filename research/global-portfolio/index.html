Academic objective

The project is presented as a decision framework connecting macroeconomic assumptions with asset-allocation choices—not as a current portfolio recommendation or track record.

Decision process

  1. Define the macroeconomic view and investment horizon.
  2. Translate that view into candidate asset classes.
  3. Set diversification and risk constraints.
  4. Choose a benchmark and rebalancing rule.
  5. Document assumptions and stress scenarios.

The public case stops at process because the underlying weights and risk outputs were not found.

Thesis invalidation

Macro conditions diverging from the stated assumptions, correlations rising during stress, or concentration overwhelming diversification would require the allocation rationale to be revisited.
