PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT / ACADEMIC / HYPOTHETICAL

$300,000 global portfolio

An academic allocation exercise connecting macroeconomic assumptions with diversification, risk constraints, and a documented portfolio rationale.

Research question
How should macro views translate into a diversified allocation?
Method
Asset allocation and risk optimization
Notional value
$300,000 hypothetical
Capital
No real funds
Last portfolio review
Jul 27, 2026

Academic objective

The project was designed to connect macroeconomic analysis with asset-allocation decisions rather than to claim live performance or investment authority.

Decision process

  1. Form a macroeconomic view.
  2. Translate that view into asset-allocation choices.
  3. Evaluate diversification and portfolio risk.
  4. Document the rationale and trade-offs.

Constraints and risks

The exercise depends on assumptions about economic conditions, correlations, volatility, and implementation. Those assumptions can change, and a classroom allocation does not establish future performance.

What would invalidate the rationale

Macro conditions moving differently from the stated assumptions, correlations rising during stress, or concentration overwhelming diversification would require the allocation rationale to be revisited.

Evidence boundary

The current public artifact documents the project’s method and educational boundary. It does not present a live account, audited return series, brokerage connection, or real-money track record.
