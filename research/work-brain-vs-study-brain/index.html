CFA Level I · The honest part

Work brain and study brain are not the same brain.

Same day. Same person. Totally different fuel left in the tank.

A line slopes from “Sharp” at 9 AM—the internship—to “Fumes” and “empty” at 8 PM—the books.

Still figuring out how to show up with something left in the tank.

What it records

The visual does not offer a study formula. It records the practical challenge of protecting enough attention for evening learning after a full workday. The useful question is not whether motivation exists at 8 PM, but what routine makes consistent study possible when energy is lower.
