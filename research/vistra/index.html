Investment thesis

The report connects Q1 2026 results, the anticipated Cogentrix close and synergy update, and another potential hyperscaler agreement with Vistra’s diversified nuclear, gas, solar, storage, and retail footprint.

Valuation

The $208 target blends a $193.79 DCF value at 60% and a $229.31 peer-relative value at 40%. The DCF uses a 13.0% WACC, a 15.5x exit EV/EBITDA multiple, and $9.2B of FY2028 estimated EBITDA.

Catalysts

  • Quarterly operating results and cash-flow evidence.
  • Cogentrix close and synergy communication.
  • Potential hyperscaler demand agreements.

Risks

  • FERC and contract regulatory risk.
  • Supplier, tariff, integration, and nuclear-outage execution risk.
  • Valuation and sentiment risk after a large prior price move.

What would invalidate the thesis

Weakening operating evidence, failed integration, materially lower cash-flow expectations, or catalysts that do not translate into durable earnings would challenge the report’s mechanism. The follow-up visual remains an undated source artifact and is not presented as current evidence.
