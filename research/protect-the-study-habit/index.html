CFA Level I · How I stay in it

I lowered the bar. That’s why I’m still going.

Not every study night is a good one. The trick is making sure there is a study night at all.

Thirty circles distinguish “a real, focused night” from “just showed up—still counts.”

30 nights. Not every one was good. Not one was skipped.

The reflection shared on LinkedIn

I stopped trying to study well after work. I started trying to just study at all.

The mistake was expecting the same sharp focus at 8 PM that I bring to the internship at 10 AM. That brain’s already spent.

So the bar changed. Some nights it’s real, focused work. Some nights it’s just showing up and keeping the streak alive. Both count.

Lowering the standard on the tired days is the only reason I haven’t quit the tired days entirely.

If you’ve juggled work and a big exam—did you push for quality every night, or just protect the habit? Curious what actually held up.
