Start with $10m of fictional year-zero free cash flow to the firm. Change five-year growth and discount assumptions. The result is operating value—not equity value or a share price.

How is this calculated?

FCFFₜ = $10m × (1 + forecast growth)ᵗ. Each forecast cash flow is discounted by (1 + discount rate)ᵗ. Terminal value at year 5 = FCFF₅ × (1 + terminal growth) ÷ (discount rate − terminal growth), then discounted five years. Operating value is the sum.

The sliders illustrate sensitivity, not economically validated assumptions. Reinvestment and operating fundamentals are not separately modeled. Debt, non-operating assets, taxes, and share counts are not reconciled into equity value.
